How to play

  1. Enter a player name.
  2. Read the target output RPM and rotation direction.
  3. Select a driven gear and 0, 1 or 2 idler gears.
  4. Press Engage & test to check your arrangement.
  5. Earn points for a correct gear train. Build a streak for bonus points.

Scores are stored locally in this browser and automatically expire after 90 days. They are not shared between devices or browsers.
