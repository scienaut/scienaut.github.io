1D Tolerance Stack-Up Analyzer

Build a signed linear dimensional chain and evaluate its functional limits using worst-case and statistical Root Sum Square (RSS) methods. Enter drawing deviations separately; statistical assumptions are never applied silently.

01 · Stack setup

Define the output equation through each component's contribution direction. For a clearance stack, additive dimensions increase clearance and subtractive dimensions reduce it.

02 · Component dimensions

Enter the nominal drawing size and signed deviations. Example: 25 +0.20 / −0.10 means upper deviation +0.20 and lower deviation −0.10. Use the direction column to describe how each size affects the functional output. Read the short explanation under each heading before entering data.

Statistical data: measured/process sigma uses the entered absolute process mean and standard deviation. Tolerance-based estimate assumes a centred normal process over the drawing limits (±3σ); its mean is the midpoint of the limits. RSS assumes independent contributors.

How the analyzer calculates sigma from raw measurements

When “Raw measured dimensions” is selected, enter at least two measurements for that component, separated by commas, spaces or semicolons. The analyzer calculates the sample mean first, then calculates sample standard deviation using n − 1.

Sample mean: x̄ = Σxᵢ / n
Sample sigma: s = √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1)]

Here xᵢ is each measured dimension, x̄ is the sample mean and n is the number of measurements. The resulting mean and sigma are then used in the signed stack mean and RSS calculation. At least two values are required; more representative, stable-process measurements are preferable.
