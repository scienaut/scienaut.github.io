Gear dimensions

mₜ = mₙ / cos β ; tan αₜ = tan αₙ / cos β
d = mₜ z ; dᵦ = d cos αₜ
dₐ = d + 2mₙ(1 + x) ; d𝒻 = d − 2mₙ(1.25 − x)
inv(α) = tan α − α (α in radians)

Basic rack addendum 1.0m, dedendum 1.25m and clearance 0.25m are explicit V1 assumptions. Root fillet, cutter tip radius, undercut, tip thickness and interference require additional verification.

Tooth-root bending screening

Lewis approximation only; not ISO 6336-3 / IS 19153 Part 3. No safety factor is declared unless the user enters material allowables.
σF ≈ Ft × Kₐ Kᵥ Kβ Kα / (b mₙ Y)
Y ≈ 0.484 − 2.87/zᵥ ; zᵥ = z/cos³β

Hertz contact screening

Simplified line-contact screening only; not ISO 6336-2 / IS 19153 Part 2 pitting rating.
ZE = √{1/[π((1−ν₁²)/E₁ + (1−ν₂²)/E₂)]}
σH ≈ ZE √[Ft,design/(b d₁) × (u+1)/u]

Material selection

Editable nominal elastic-property presets. Strength allowables are intentionally not auto-filled; they depend on heat treatment, hardness, cleanliness, material quality, size and life.

Nominal E, ν and density are preliminary reference properties only. Verify exact grade and condition using material certificates and applicable BIS/EN/ASTM specifications and ISO 6336-5 before approval.

Standards basis

ASME Y14.7.1-1971 (R1998) is marked by ASME as historical reference only; it covers gear drawing data conventions and is not a current ASME-approved gear strength code. V1 does not claim ASME strength compliance.
BIS published catalogue lists IS 19153 Parts 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6:2025 as identical under dual numbering to ISO 6336 Parts 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6. IS 2535 Parts 1 and 2 correspond to ISO 53 and ISO 54. Confirm amendments and current status before contractual use.
