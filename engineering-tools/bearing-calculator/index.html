ENGINEERING EQUATIONS

Formula guide and references

REFERENCE NOTES
Equivalent dynamic loadP = XFr + YFa
P = X Fᵣ + Y Fₐ

Combined radial and axial loading is represented by an equivalent constant load for fatigue-life calculations. X and Y depend on bearing type, load ratio and manufacturer method. Use exact catalogue factors for the bearing designation and arrangement.

Reference: ISO 281:2007; SKF Rolling Bearings Catalogue; Shigley’s Mechanical Engineering Design, rolling-contact bearings.

Basic dynamic rating lifeL₁₀ = (C/P)ᵖ
L₁₀ = (C/P)ᵖ · 10⁶ revolutions

Basic rating life is the statistical life associated with 90% reliability under conventional conditions. The life exponent is 3 for ball bearings and 10/3 for roller bearings. It does not account for all lubrication, contamination or installation effects.

Reference: ISO 281:2007; NSK technical guide.

Rating life in operating hoursL₁₀h = L₁₀ × 10⁶/(60n)
L₁₀h = (10⁶ / 60n) (C/P)ᵖ

The revolution-based rating life is converted to hours using constant rotational speed n in revolutions per minute. For variable speed or load duty cycles, use the appropriate equivalent-load and cycle weighting method instead of a single constant operating point.

Reference: ISO 281:2007; NSK Dynamic Load Ratings and Fatigue Life.

Equivalent static load and static safetyP₀ = X₀Fr + Y₀Fa; s₀ = C₀/P₀
P₀ = X₀ Fᵣ + Y₀ Fₐs₀ = C₀ / P₀

The equivalent static load is used with the basic static rating to assess permanent deformation risk at rolling contacts. X₀ and Y₀ depend on the bearing type and catalogue method. Required static safety is application-specific; no universal pass threshold is hard-coded.

Reference: ISO 76:2006; SKF Rolling Bearings Catalogue.

Modified rating lifeLₙₘ = a₁ aISO L₁₀
Lₙₘ = a₁ aISO L₁₀

The modified-life method can account for reliability, lubrication condition, contamination and fatigue load limit. This release calculates the a₁ reliability-only adjustment; it does not calculate aISO. Do not treat the reliability-only result as a full ISO 281 modified rating life.

Reference: ISO 281:2007; SKF Rolling Bearings Catalogue.

Bearing mean diameter and viscosity ratiodₘ = (d + D)/2; κ = ν/ν₁
dₘ = (d + D)/2κ = ν/ν₁

The mean diameter is a geometry parameter used in bearing speed and lubricant-viscosity guidance. The viscosity ratio compares actual lubricant operating viscosity with the reference viscosity for the bearing and operating speed. This release does not evaluate κ or lubrication adequacy.

Reference: SKF Rolling Bearings Catalogue, lubrication guidance.

DESIGN REVIEW & APPROVAL

Before bearing procurement

Review all calculations in accordance with your organisation’s engineering policies and design validation process. Verify bearing dimensions, shaft and housing fits, radial and axial loads, shock loading, required life, static safety, speed limits, lubrication, operating temperature, sealing, alignment and installation requirements.

The displayed results are preliminary engineering assessments. They do not constitute final design approval or guarantee component performance. Verify manufacturer ratings and catalogue revisions, obtain the required engineering and procurement approvals, and confirm that the selected bearing is suitable for the complete application before proceeding with ordering or installation.
