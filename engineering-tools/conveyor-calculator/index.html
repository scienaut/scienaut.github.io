STANDARD · CONVEYOR RESISTANCE

ISO 5048:1989

Current published edition at check: Edition 2, published 1989-09; ISO shows it confirmed on 2026-06-22.

Scope: operating power and tensile forces for belt conveyors with carrying idlers and smooth, patternless belts. The application’s simplified skid-plate model and current force approximation do not claim ISO 5048 conformance.

Official ISO record ↗
STANDARD · TEXTILE BELTS

ISO 14890:2026

Edition 3: published 2026-02. Applies to rubber- or plastics-covered textile conveyor belts for general surface use on flat or troughed idlers; excludes light conveyor belts described in ISO 21183-1.

Use the selected belt supplier’s datasheet for mass, tensile rating and allowable working tension.

Official ISO record ↗
STANDARD · BEARING DYNAMIC LIFE

ISO 281:2007

Current published edition at check: Edition 2, reviewed and confirmed in 2021. ISO notes a replacement is expected in the coming months as ISO/DIS 281 proceeds.

The calculator uses the basic L10 form with user-entered manufacturer ratings and X/Y factors; modified life and application corrections are not implemented.

Official ISO record ↗
STANDARD · BEARING STATIC LOAD

ISO 76:2006 + Amd 1:2017

ISO lists the 2006 edition with its 2017 amendment as published; the ISO record indicates revision is planned. Static rating methods and actual manufacturer factors must be checked for the selected bearing.

Official ISO record ↗
