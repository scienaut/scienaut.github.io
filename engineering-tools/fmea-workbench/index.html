Risk thresholds

Low: ≤ low maxMedium: above low to medium maxHigh: above medium max

Thresholds are organisation-defined examples. The maximum theoretical RPN is 1000 for a 1–10 rating scale.

Rating scale

V1 uses integer ratings from 1 to 10 for Severity, Occurrence and Detection. Rating definitions should be agreed and documented by the organisation before use.

Project information

Risk interpretation safeguard

RPN = Severity × Occurrence × Detection. A lower RPN does not necessarily mean a failure is acceptable. Review high-severity effects independently and apply any mandatory escalation rules required by your organisation, customer or applicable standard. This V1 implements configurable RPN bands, not an AI safety approval or an AIAG-VDA Action Priority table.

Export notes

Excel, PDF and DOCX export libraries are loaded from public CDNs and therefore require an internet connection when exporting. Project editing and browser-local saving work without a server. Data is not uploaded to a cloud service by this application.
