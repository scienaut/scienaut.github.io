σb = M·Do/(2I) ; τ = T·Do/(2J) ; σa = Fx/A

The fatigue check is a simplified screening calculation, not a full fatigue-life prediction. The preliminary equivalent diameter is not a substitute for iterative stepped-shaft design.

References and limitations

Material references: ISO 683 series, BIS IS 1570 / IS 2062 where applicable, ASTM A29/A29M, ASM / MatWeb property references, manufacturer datasheets and mill test certificates. Engineering methods: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design; ASME B106.1M may be relevant to transmission shafting. Verify current editions and applicability. No blanket standards compliance is claimed.

V1.0 uses linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam assumptions, small deflections, point loads and two radial simple supports. It does not calculate bearing life, critical speed, dynamic response, gear forces, full fatigue life, notch sensitivity, residual/thermal stress or arbitrary 3D geometry. Verify all critical properties and results independently.
