Motor & Gearbox Sizing

Convert joint-side torque and speed requirements into preliminary motor and gearbox specifications. Enter calculated loads from Kinematics & Dynamics or measured design requirements. All ratings remain user-entered or manufacturer-sourced.

01 · Design inputs

For each joint, enter output-side continuous torque (preferably RMS over the duty cycle), peak torque, and maximum output speed. If you copy values from the dynamics tab, ensure they are joint-side values and include gravity, payload, friction and acceleration as applicable.
Example values are illustrative only. Replace them with calculated joint loads and manufacturer data.

Enter joint loads and calculate to see motor and gearbox requirements.
Interpretation: motor torque is gearbox output torque divided by ratio and gearbox efficiency. Motor speed is output speed multiplied by ratio. Power is calculated from torque × angular speed. The service factor is applied to the gearbox torque rating requirement, not silently added to the physics torque.
Important: this is a preliminary sizing calculator, not a vendor selection engine. It does not infer motor thermal capability, peak duration, drive current, bus voltage, inertia reflected through the gearbox, gearbox life, backlash, stiffness, bearing loads or regenerative braking. Confirm these against the selected manufacturer's datasheets and duty-cycle limits.
